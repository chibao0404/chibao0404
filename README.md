## 🎧 [SGMSE+ Conformer](https://github.com/chibao0404/sgmse-conformer)

**Diffusion-based Speech Enhancement Research Extension**

`Python` `PyTorch` `NCSN++` `Conformer` `Speech Enhancement`

A research extension of the upstream SGMSE+ framework exploring a Conformer-style bottleneck inside the NCSN++ score network.

### What I worked on

- Extended the NCSN++ architecture by replacing its mid-block self-attention layer with a **Conformer-style bottleneck**.
- Implemented and sanity-checked the **ResNet → Conformer → ResNet** bottleneck path while preserving compatibility with the surrounding SGMSE diffusion pipeline.
- Worked with speech-enhancement evaluation tooling including **PESQ, ESTOI, SI-SDR, SI-SIR, and SI-SAR**.

➡️ **[View repository](https://github.com/chibao0404/sgmse-conformer)**
